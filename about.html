Introduction

My Approach

Three Principles That Shape Every Project I Take On.

Every book is different, but the way I approach a marketing project rests on a small number of ideas that have proven themselves over the years.

01

Understand the Book First

Before any marketing work begins, I take the time to understand the book, the author behind it, and the readers it is written for. A clear understanding of these three things informs every decision that follows, from positioning and metadata to advertising creative and outreach.

02

Plan Before You Promote

Random activity rarely produces lasting results. I prefer to work from a clear plan with defined goals, a sensible timeline, and measurable outcomes. A plan does not limit creativity. It gives creativity a purpose and a direction.

03

Measure, Adjust, Continue

Book marketing is a long game. I monitor what is working, adjust what is not, and continue to refine campaigns based on real evidence rather than assumptions. The most meaningful results tend to come from consistency, not short bursts of activity.

Professional Values

The Values That Guide My Work.

These are the principles I return to whenever a project becomes complex and a decision needs to be made in the author's best interest.

Honesty

Authors deserve a clear view of what marketing can and cannot do. I would rather set realistic expectations and exceed them than offer promises I have no reliable way of keeping. Honesty is the foundation of every working relationship I build.

Craft

Book marketing is a craft, not a shortcut. The small details, a well-written description, a thoughtful keyword, a carefully chosen image, often make the difference between a book that sells quietly and a book that finds momentum. I care about those details.

Respect

Respect for the author, respect for the reader, and respect for the book. Outreach is carried out ethically, reviews are requested rather than demanded, and campaigns are built around real audiences rather than inflated numbers.

Patience

Author careers are built over time. I plan with that longer horizon in mind, favouring sustainable approaches over short-term tactics that rarely survive past a single campaign.

Clarity

Clear plans, clear communication, and clear reporting. Authors should always understand what is being done on their behalf, why it is being done, and what the results mean for the next step.

My Commitment

Helping Authors Market Their Books, Strategically and Sustainably.

My commitment to the authors I work with is straightforward. I will take the time to understand your book, plan a marketing approach that reflects its genre and audience, and carry out the work with the same care I would bring to a book of my own.

I will communicate clearly about what is realistic, measure performance honestly, and recommend adjustments when the evidence suggests a different direction. The aim is always long-term author visibility, not a brief spike that fades the following week.
