Prefer a Custom Arrangement?

Not every book fits neatly into a fixed package, and that is perfectly acceptable. If you have a specific combination of services in mind, or a particular challenge you would like to focus on, we can shape an engagement around your actual needs rather than a predetermined list.

Individual services are also available on their own. Many authors begin with a single focused project, such as a book description rewrite or an Amazon Ads setup, and expand from there as the working relationship develops.
