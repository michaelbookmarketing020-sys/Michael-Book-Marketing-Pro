Services

Marketing Services Built for Authors and Their Books.

A complete range of book marketing services, from cover presentation and metadata to advertising, outreach, publicity, and long-term strategy. Each service is offered with care, planning, and an understanding of what helps a book find its readers.

An Overview

A Complete Marketing Toolkit.

Book marketing works best when every element supports the others. A thoughtful cover draws attention, strong metadata helps readers discover the book, well-targeted advertising brings in the right audience, and consistent outreach keeps momentum alive.

The services below are grouped into seven categories. Authors can work with me on a specific service, a focused combination, or a full marketing programme depending on the stage of their book and the goals they have in mind.

Category 01

Book Branding & Creative Design

The visual presentation of a book is often the first and most important impression a reader has. Covers, mockups, promotional graphics, and author branding work together to communicate the tone, genre, and quality of your work. This category covers the creative foundation every book marketing campaign depends on.

A strong visual identity makes advertising more effective, builds recognition for your author name, and gives booksellers, reviewers, and readers confidence in what they are looking at. Every element is designed to reflect your book and the readers it is written for.

Book Promotion & Advertising

Paid promotion is one of the most reliable ways to put a book in front of relevant readers. From Amazon Ads to Facebook and Instagram campaigns, well-planned advertising creates the visibility that a book needs during a launch and the momentum it needs to sustain sales afterwards.

Every campaign begins with audience research, clear objectives, and a realistic budget. Creative is developed around the book, campaigns are monitored throughout their run, and adjustments are made based on real performance data rather than assumptions.

Category 03

Reader Outreach & Audience Development

Readers are the foundation of every successful book. Reaching the right ones requires direct outreach to book bloggers, influencers, newsletters, reading communities, and genre-specific audiences. This category covers the technical and strategic work that helps your book surface in relevant searches and stand up well against competing titles in its category.
