A Note from Michael

Marketing Built Around the Book, the Author, and the Reader.

I work with authors who care about their books and want them to find the right readers. Every project I take on begins with listening: understanding the story, the author behind it, and the audience it is written for.

My role is to translate that understanding into a clear, considered marketing plan. From cover presentation and metadata to advertising, outreach, and long-term visibility, I focus on the details that help a book stand out in a crowded market.

Book marketing is a craft that rewards patience, consistency, and strategy. My work is grounded in all three.
